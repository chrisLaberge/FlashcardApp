**App name (30 max):** StompaCard

**Short description (80 max):**
Learn words with flashcards, read your own PDFs, and grow a pet.

**Full description (4000 max):**
StompaCard is a flashcard app for learning languages, built around reading what you actually want to read.

STUDY A LITTLE EACH DAY
Cards come back just before you would forget them. Say the word, flip the card, and tell the app how it went. Cards move up through study levels from recognising a word to producing it.

ADD WORDS AS YOU MEET THEM
Type a word, or tap one while you read. A meaning, a picture, a sound and an example sentence can come with it. AI explanations give you a clear definition, grammar notes and examples for any card.

READ YOUR OWN PDFs
Add a book or article as a PDF. Words you do not know yet are highlighted, words already in your deck are marked softly, and every book shows how much of it you already understand. Tap any word to look it up and add it as a card.

KEEP A PET
Studying levels up your pet and earns gems. Open cases, unlock hats, glasses and scarves, and watch your pet grow. A daily streak earns more.

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
Streaks, daily goals, achievements and a calendar of everything you studied. Add friends with a friend code and see how you rank on the leaderboard (you can opt out).

YOURS TO KEEP
Your decks and progress live on your phone. Export a backup, with your PDFs if you like, and restore it on any device.

Works with many languages, including Russian, Chinese, Japanese, Spanish, French and German.
