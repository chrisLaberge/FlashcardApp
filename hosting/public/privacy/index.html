StompaCard Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

StompaCard is a flashcard app for learning languages. This page explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and how you can delete it. Contact: christopher.laberge.42@gmail.com

What stays on your phone

Your decks, cards, pictures, study progress, review history and settings are stored on your phone. They are not uploaded unless you use one of the online features described below. You can export a backup file yourself with the Export button.

Your account

StompaCard needs an account to use. Your decks, cards and progress still live on your phone; the account is used for sign-in, friends and the leaderboard.

What an account stores

You can sign in with email and password or with Google. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We receive your email address and, for Google sign-in, your Google account name. We never see your password.

If you set up an online profile for friends and the leaderboard, we store:

  • your username, friend code, level, XP and streak;
  • for each day you studied: the date, number of reviews, number correct, time spent and XP earned (not which cards or words);
  • your friends list and friend requests.

Your friends can see your username, level, XP and streak. Unless you turn it off (Progress, Friends, Global leaderboard), everyone can see your username, level, XP and streak on the global leaderboard. Your email address is never shown to other users.

Features that send data to our server

  • Translation: when you ask for a translation, the text you typed is sent to our server and on to Google Cloud Translation.
  • Picture search: your search term is sent to our server and on to Pixabay. Pictures you choose are downloaded from Pixabay, resized, and stored in our cloud storage so they can be shown on your cards. Stored pictures have a web address that is not listed anywhere but is not password-protected.
  • Word explanations and lookups: when you tap a word or ask for an explanation, the word, its meaning on your card and (for lookups) the sentence it was in are sent to our server and on to Anthropic, which writes the explanation. Answers may be kept on our server for up to a week so the same question is not asked twice. Nothing else from your cards is sent.
  • PDFs: PDF files you add are read on your phone and are not uploaded.
  • Spoken pronunciation: for most languages, speech is produced on your phone by Android's speech engine. For some languages the word may be sent to our server to generate audio.

Technical logs

Our server runs on Google Cloud, which keeps standard request logs (such as time and IP address) for security and troubleshooting.

What we do not do

StompaCard has no ads and no third-party analytics. We do not sell your data.

Who processes your data

We use Google Cloud and Firebase (hosting, database, storage, sign-in, translation), Pixabay (pictures) and Anthropic (word explanations). Their own privacy policies apply to the data they handle.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Account, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your sign-in account, username, friends, uploaded daily summaries and your global leaderboard entry. Decks and progress on your phone are kept; uninstalling the app removes those.
  • Online data only: Progress, Friends, Delete my online data removes your profile, friends and stats but keeps your sign-in account.
  • By email: write to christopher.laberge.42@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we will delete your data.

Deleted data is removed from our database right away. Copies in routine backups or logs are removed on their normal schedule.

Children

StompaCard is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top.
