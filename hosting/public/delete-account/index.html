Delete your StompaCard account

In the app

  1. Open StompaCard and go to the Account tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account, then confirm.

This permanently deletes your sign-in account, username, friends, uploaded daily summaries and your leaderboard entry, and erases the decks and progress on that phone. If the app says you need to sign in again first, sign in and repeat the steps.

By email

If you cannot open the app, write to christopher.laberge.42@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, and we will delete your account and online data.

What happens to data on the phone

Deleting the account in the app also erases the decks, cards, progress and saved PDFs on that phone. Export a backup first if you want to keep them. If you delete by email, uninstalling the app removes what is on the phone. Deleted online data is removed right away; copies in routine backups or logs are removed on their normal schedule.
